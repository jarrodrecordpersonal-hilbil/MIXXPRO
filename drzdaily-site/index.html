My story / The real origin

I built this because my own routine got complicated.

At 50, I was lifting, following bloodwork, working with doctors and trying to sort through dozens of supplements and conflicting advice. I went from about 245 pounds to about 205, and I've maintained my weight.

That weight change involved medical care, including weight-management medication, alongside training and lifestyle changes. It was not caused by DRZ Daily. This program did not exist then.

What I want to share isn't a miracle stack. It's a straightforward way to organize the journey, ask better questions and stop guessing.

01 / The framework

Six steps. Ninety days. A clear starting point.

All six starting steps fit inside the first 90 days. My own health journey took about 18 months. The course gives you a place to start, not a promise to repeat my results on a deadline.

01Weeks 1–2

Map your health.

Build one accurate list of your prescriptions and supplements. Note your goals, habits and questions for your clinician.

Inventory + health snapshot
02Weeks 2–3

Measure what matters.

Put the basics in one place: sleep, movement, and relevant existing bloodwork. A clinician decides whether any new lab tests make sense.

Personalized baseline + labs
03Weeks 3–5

Stabilize your inputs.

Build repeatable actions for sleep, food, movement and recovery. Aim for consistency before adding anything new.

Daily foundations
04Weeks 5–8

Personalize the plan.

Simplify your supplement inventory and flag duplication or questions for a clinician. Only your prescriber changes prescription medications.

Individualized action plan
05Weeks 8–11

Adapt with feedback.

Check your weekly progress. Adjust habits that are not working and bring medical questions to a qualified professional.

Weekly check-ins
06Week 12

Recheck and sustain.

Compare your day-90 snapshot with day one and create a plan for continuing. Your doctor can guide any follow-up testing.

90-day review + next steps

05 / Good questions

A long-term approach starts with better questions.

Does the reset put my body into homeostasis?

Homeostasis refers to the body's ongoing self-regulation, not a clinical finish line that a course can promise. DRZ Daily proposes measurable habits and risk-factor improvements; it does not promise to "reset" the body or reverse aging.

Does everyone get the same supplements?

No. Supplements are optional and should be evaluated for evidence, safety, dose and duplication. Prescription medicines are never changed based on the course alone.

Do I need blood tests?

Not for the digital education program. If you choose a clinician-supported program, the clinician decides which tests are medically appropriate. Lab collection may take place at a local participating facility.

Does DRZ Daily replace my physician?

No. General education is not a diagnosis or treatment. Clinical services, if launched, must be delivered by appropriately licensed professionals and documented through a secure clinical system.

How does an 18-month story fit with a 90-day program?

My progress and the refinement of my personal routine unfolded over approximately 18 months. The DRZ Daily starter program organizes the first 90 days into six steps. It does not promise similar results in 90 days, or at any particular time.
