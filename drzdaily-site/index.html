What the deeper cholesterol testing revealed +

Better total cholesterol was not the whole story. My LDL particle number increased from 1,261 to 1,670 nmol/L (+32.4%), while small LDL particles rose from 227 to 461 nmol/L (+103.1%). These are real concerns to review with my clinician—not wins to hide or a reason to promise that everyone needs the same tests.

03 / 05 — The 90-day system

Six simple steps.
One direction.

The six-step framework, plus a transparent ingredient-by-ingredient look at the supplements in my personal routine. The final DRZ Daily product, if developed, will be simpler.

01

Map

Write down your goals, habits, prescriptions and supplements.

02

Measure

Review useful existing numbers and ask your clinician whether new tests are needed.

03

Stabilize

Build a consistent foundation for sleep, movement, nutrition and recovery.

04

Personalize

Keep what helps, simplify what doesn't, and discuss medical changes with your doctor.

05

Adjust

Track what you can sustain and adapt your daily habits over time.

06

Recheck

Review your first 90 days and plan follow-up testing only when appropriate.

The goal is progress you can understand and sustain. No requirement to purchase supplements, change prescriptions, or achieve a number on a deadline.
