01 / The framework

Six stages. One thoughtful path.

An 18-month journey cannot be compressed into a quick fix. The first 90 days build a baseline and daily foundations; the next phases allow time to learn, adjust, and sustain.

01Weeks 0–2

Map your health.

Gather medications, supplements, symptoms, goals, habits, and relevant history. Check medication safety, possible duplications, and what needs professional review.

Inventory + health snapshot
02Weeks 2–4

Measure what matters.

Record relevant health indicators and choose a useful baseline. A licensed clinician determines whether lab testing is appropriate and which tests matter.

Personalized baseline + labs
03Months 1–3

Stabilize your inputs.

Build sustainable routines for sleep, nutrition, activity, and recovery. The first 90 days are a foundation, not a guaranteed transformation.

Daily foundations
04Months 3–6

Personalize the plan.

Revisit priorities after the foundation is established. Review medications with the prescriber and evaluate supplements individually for evidence, safety, and duplication.

Individualized action plan
05Months 6–12

Adapt with feedback.

Track meaningful behaviors and risk indicators over months, not days. Make adjustments with an appropriate professional rather than adding more products by default.

Weekly check-ins
06Months 12–18

Recheck and sustain.

Compare progress with the original baseline, revisit goals and medicines with clinicians as needed, and build a sustainable next-year plan. Repeat tests only when indicated.

05 / Good questions

A long-term approach starts with better questions.

Does the reset put my body into homeostasis?

Homeostasis refers to the body's ongoing self-regulation, not a clinical finish line that a course can promise. DRZ Daily proposes measurable habits and risk-factor improvements; it does not promise to "reset" the body or reverse aging.

Does everyone get the same supplements?

No. Supplements are optional and should be evaluated for evidence, safety, dose and duplication. Prescription medicines are never changed based on the course alone.

Do I need blood tests?

Not for the digital education program. If you choose a clinician-supported program, the clinician decides which tests are medically appropriate. Lab collection may take place at a local participating facility.

Does DRZ Daily replace my physician?

No. General education is not a diagnosis or treatment. Clinical services, if launched, must be delivered by appropriately licensed professionals and documented through a secure clinical system.

Why 18 months if the entry program is 90 days?

The founder's journey took 18 months. The 90-day entry product teaches foundations and helps people organize a baseline. Later stages are optional and tailored, with monitoring and clinically indicated care over a longer period. No fixed timeline or outcome is guaranteed.

Less noise.
Better direction.

An 18-month philosophy, beginning with a 90-day plan. Real progress takes observation, sustainable habits and appropriate medical guidance—not a universal stack or overnight reset.
